Frequently asked questions

Is real money involved?

No. Casino Academy uses play money only. Chips cannot be bought, won, redeemed or cashed out.

What is free and what is Premium?

Blackjack, High Card War, Roulette and Craps are free, with one free card-counting session per day. Premium (annual subscription or one-time lifetime purchase) unlocks the other six games, the Craps coach, full progress charts and unlimited counting practice. Premium never sells chips.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Casino Academy, tap Go Premium on the home screen, then tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel the annual subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Casino Academy, then tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal. Premium stays active until the paid period ends.

I ran out of chips.

When your bankroll reaches $0 the table offers a free $200 reload. You can also carry chips from another game when you start a new one.

Can I resume a game I left?

Yes. When you tap Play the table, the app offers to resume your previous game for that table.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Premium can back up your history to your own iCloud so it follows you to a new device. Without Premium, history is stored on your phone only. Deleting the app removes locally stored data.

Is the strategy accurate?

The coach uses standard basic strategy and exact odds for the rules shown on your table. Casino rules vary, so always check the rules at the table you play.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
