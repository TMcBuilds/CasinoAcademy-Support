Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Casino Academy ("the app") is published by MCaptures LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect your personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. We do not receive any data about you or how you use the app, and we do not sell or share data.

What is stored on your device

The app saves your settings, your play-money bankroll, saved tables and your game history and statistics (such as hands played and decisions made). This stays on your device and is used only to run the app and show your progress.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you have Premium and iCloud Drive turned on, the app can copy your game history to the app's private container in your own iCloud account so it follows you to a new device. This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see or access it. You can turn the backup off in the app's Progress screen.

Purchases

Premium purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether Premium is active on your account.

Play money

All chips are play money with no cash value. The app offers no real-money gambling, and chips cannot be bought, won or cashed out.

Children

The app is rated 17+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the app. To remove iCloud backup data, delete the app's data in Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
